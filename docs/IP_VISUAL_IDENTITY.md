# Gangnam Dream IP Visual Identity

Updated: 2026-07-10

## Why This Exists

Gangnam Dream currently has atmosphere, readable portraits, and many story assets, but it does not yet have one image that a player can recall after closing the game. The rooftop key art sells isolation; it does not sell the cast. The start menu sells rules and save slots; it does not sell desire, danger, or attachment.

This document sits above `GANGNAM_INK_ART_DIRECTION.md`. Gangnam Ink is the film stock. This document defines what must be photographed through it.

The commercial test is simple:

- Can a player identify the game from one cropped face, one prop, or one window frame?
- Can a fan describe why they prefer Jiyeon, Daeun, Jaehyuk, or Sangchul without quoting a stat bonus?
- Can Minjun be recognized in Gray, White, and Black without changing him into three different people?
- Would a character stand, postcard, pin, or lenticular print still make sense without the game UI around it?

If the answer is no, adding more backgrounds or event portraits does not solve the problem.

## Core Image: A Life Seen Through Glass

The recurring visual symbol is **glass that separates Minjun from the life he wants and reflects the person he is becoming**.

- The tiny frosted goshiwon window is the beginning.
- Subway doors, office partitions, cafe windows, investment screens, Jiyeon's car windows, and Gangnam towers repeat the same shape at different social levels.
- Reflections reveal Minjun. They do not display a morality score.
- Gray reflection: tired, unresolved, almost colorless.
- White reflection: clear face, visible people behind him, clean air and readable distance.
- Black reflection: successful silhouette, swallowed facial detail, money and metal remain unnaturally sharp.

This is the game's signature, not a decorative filter. Use it in the title key art, chapter cards, ending recaps, Steam capsules, gallery frames, and merchandise.

## Flagship Cast

Only these six characters lead launch branding. Supporting characters remain important in play, but adding everyone to key art weakens recognition.

### Character Gate

Every flagship character asset must pass all five gates:

1. **64 px silhouette:** hair, shoulders, and clothing block remain distinguishable.
2. **Prop ownership:** the signature object cannot be reassigned to another lead.
3. **Expression grammar:** the same emotional beat is performed differently by each character.
4. **Scene ownership:** at least one launch/demo scene could not be transferred to another character without collapsing.
5. **Outfit continuity:** portrait, CG, gallery thumbnail, and promotional crop agree on the scene outfit.

Conventional attractiveness is not enough. A beautiful face without owned behavior, silhouette, and material language remains generic.

## Heroine Polarity

Jiyeon and Daeun are not difficulty options and not a rich/poor reskin.

- **Jiyeon** occupies reflective, controlled spaces. She usually holds the stronger line of sight, enters with negative space around her, and reveals warmth through a brief break in posture.
- **Daeun** occupies handled, lived-in spaces. Her gestures touch small objects and practical needs; warmth is direct, while hurt appears as reduced eye contact rather than glamour lighting.
- Jiyeon's visual question is: "How much of this was calculated?"
- Daeun's visual question is: "When did this ordinary kindness become irreplaceable?"

Their colors remain restrained. Character identity comes from material, silhouette, staging, and sound rather than assigning one woman blue and the other orange.

## Minjun Is The Collectible Transformation

Minjun must stop reading as an empty male player insert. He is one consistent person whose face, posture, and surroundings accumulate the player's choices.

- **Gray:** centered but slightly closed posture; eye line avoids the horizon; matte crewneck; concrete reflection.
- **White:** same clothes and face where context permits; shoulders open; eyes meet another person; reflections include human presence; texture becomes clear, not golden.
- **Black:** expensive clothing only when earned by context; still the same hairline and facial proportions; posture becomes controlled; eyes stop searching; glass reflection loses facial detail.

The preferred merchandise object is a three-state lenticular portrait or triptych, not three unrelated costume variants.

## Title Key Art Contract

The launch image must be character-led and readable at Steam capsule scale.

### Composition

- 16:9 textless master, 1920x1080 minimum.
- Minjun in the foreground, face visible in a dark window reflection. Do not use only a back view.
- Jiyeon belongs to the cold Gangnam/car-glass side of the same physical composition.
- Daeun belongs to the lived-in convenience-store/window-light side.
- Gangnam exists beyond the glass as pressure, not as a tourism skyline.
- Father may appear only as a faint secondary reflection or owned prop, never as a sixth floating head.
- Leave a stable logo/menu safe zone on the left third and face-safe crops for 616x353, 460x215, and 231x87 Steam capsules.

### Forbidden

- A row of floating portrait busts.
- Generic blue-and-gold city wallpaper.
- Gold skyline integrated into the title.
- Back-facing Minjun as the only human signal.
- Heroine fan service unrelated to class, danger, or intimacy.
- Showing White and Black outcomes as an explicit morality-choice tutorial.

## Wordmark Contract

The current gold skyline logo is a concept asset, not production identity.

- Build a custom squared Hangul wordmark from the proportions of a goshiwon window and a high-rise facade.
- Keep one imperfect or interrupted horizontal stroke to suggest a life that does not align cleanly.
- Primary wordmark is pale concrete on dark glass. Black and light monochrome variants are required.
- English `GANGNAM DREAM` is a compact secondary lockup, not a larger separate brand.
- No coin, crown, stock arrow, luxury-car badge, or gold gradient.
- The wordmark must remain readable at 231x87 and as a monochrome enamel-pin silhouette.

## Start Screen Contract

The first interactive screen is a poster, not a dashboard.

- Full-bleed title key art with restrained parallax, rain/reflection movement, and a 6-10 second idle breath.
- Wordmark plus one short non-spoiler premise line.
- One vertical command rail: Continue, New Story, Records/Load, Options, Quit.
- Save slots, run statistics, ending counts, achievements, difficulty, and run theme live one layer deeper.
- Controller focus moves on a single axis. Confirm/cancel glyphs follow the connected controller.
- No panel may cover a flagship face.
- Start-menu music introduces Minjun's four-note motif before adding rhythm or status texture.

## Fandom Surfaces

Do not add a generic affection gallery and call the problem solved. Build repeatable character ownership:

- Relationship screen uses each lead's material and prop as a frame accent.
- Milestone CG gallery is organized by person and remembered object, not event ID.
- Character-specific UI confirmation sounds use the same audio motif as their scenes.
- Demo must contain at least one irresistible unresolved beat for Jiyeon, Daeun, Jaehyuk, and the father spine.
- Promotional crops show eyes, gestures, and owned objects. Avoid mood-only empty backgrounds.
- Optional profile cards unlock through remembered details, not encyclopedia exposition.

## Production Order

1. Lock the title key-art sketch and wordmark in grayscale.
2. Rebuild StartMenu around the poster composition before producing more generic backgrounds.
3. Create one identity sheet per flagship character: front/three-quarter silhouette, three expressions, prop, material, scene crop.
4. Audit demo CGs against the six character gates.
5. Extend owned materials into Relationship, Gallery, chapter cards, and audio leitmotifs.
6. Produce Steam capsule crops and merchandise mock tests only after the in-game title screen passes.
