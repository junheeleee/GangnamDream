# Gangnam Dream Requirements

## Architecture
- Use modular, reusable, event-driven Godot systems.
- Keep content data-driven through JSON/resource files.
- Separate event, news, investment, relationship, save/load, job, inventory, ending, and meta-progression systems.

## Event System
- Events require id, title, description, category, rarity, conditions, tags, choices, and consequences.
- Support weighted random selection, hidden events, chained events, and repetition prevention.

## News System
- Generate monthly Korean social and financial headlines.
- News should affect markets, emotions, and future events.
- Include bubbles, crashes, misleading trends, and animated breaking-news presentation.

## Investment System
- Support Korean stocks, US tech stocks, cryptocurrency, real estate, startups, and leveraged assets.
- Include volatility, bubbles, crashes, momentum, economic cycles, fear/greed, and stress-influenced decision quality.

## Relationship System
- Support family, friends, romance, coworkers, mentors, and business partners.
- Relationships should affect emotion, money, events, opportunities, betrayals, and progression.

## UI/UX
- Dark navy dashboard UI inspired by Football Manager, financial terminals, and visual novels.
- Avoid unreadable wrapping, vertical text, or blocked button input.
- Support animated stat changes, market ticker, news cards, notifications, and responsive scaling.

## Save/Load
- Multiple save slots, autosave, run history, permanent stats, and future-proof serialization.

## Meta Progression
- Unlock starting traits, hidden backgrounds, passive bonuses, rare events, secret careers, endings, and achievements.
