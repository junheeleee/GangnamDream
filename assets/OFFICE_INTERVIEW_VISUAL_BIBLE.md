This is the demo's first authored moral choice. The image must show a person evaluating Minjun, not an empty office with Minjun addressing the player.

## Location Lock

- Modest third-floor Mapo small-company interview room in daytime.
- Window and old low-rise office view are on the staff side.
- The interviewer sits on the window/staff side of the desk.
- Minjun sits on the door/candidate side.
- Frosted partition, files, fluorescent lights, and a plain dark desk communicate a small firm rather than a Gangnam corporation.
- The resume may show abstract lines only. No readable company name, logo, or personal data.

## Character Lock

### Kim Minjun

- Age 33; exact face and short messy black hair from the canonical protagonist portraits.
- Slightly inexpensive navy interview suit, wrinkled white shirt, and dark tie.
- Tired, guarded, and physically tense. This is not his later corporate-success wardrobe fantasy.

### Interviewer

- Unnamed Korean woman in her early forties.
- Practical straight chin-to-shoulder dark bob, subtle gray strands, thin rectangular glasses.
- Charcoal work blazer and muted slate blouse; no glamour, luxury jewelry, or heroine silhouette.
- One-off scene actor. Do not promote this design into a recurring main-cast portrait without a story owner.

## Camera And Acting Lock

- Natural seated eye-level three-quarter two-shot from the short side of the desk.
- Minjun occupies screen-left; the interviewer occupies screen-right.
- Interviewer gaze target: Minjun over the resume.
- Minjun gaze target: interviewer, with chin slightly lowered.
- Glasses, face angles, shoulders, resume hand, and Minjun's tense posture must support the same mutual eye line.
- Neither actor looks at the lens. The camera is an observer, not Minjun's POV.
- Both faces remain above the lower 30 percent dialogue-safe zone.

## Reject When

- The interviewer chair is empty or the interviewer is represented only by text.
- Minjun looks at the viewer instead of the interviewer.
- Either actor resembles Daeun, Jiyeon, Minseo, or another recurring woman.
- The room reads as a luxury executive office, nighttime office, cafe, or hospital.
- Minjun wears his black goshiwon sweatshirt or a polished late-game executive suit.
- The resume contains readable fake writing, a logo, or personal information.
