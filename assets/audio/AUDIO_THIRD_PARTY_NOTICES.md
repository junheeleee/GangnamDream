# Gangnam Dream Audio Notices

The shipped audio files are edited game-ready renders. Raw source libraries are
not redistributed. Every output-to-source mapping, original filename, source
hash, output hash, license, and edit summary is recorded in
`AUDIO_SOURCE_MANIFEST.json`.
